## X10 analysis terms

- Electrical conductivity and dissolved salts. FAO, *Water quality for agriculture*, chapter 2, [Salinity problems](https://www.fao.org/4/t0234e/t0234e02.htm).
- pH as a measure of acidity and alkalinity. FAO, *Water quality for agriculture*, chapter 7, [Other problems](https://www.fao.org/4/t0234e/t0234e08.htm). The explanation does not use the chapter's agronomic ranges.
- The line on the source page compares recorded endpoints for the same type of report and unit. It supplies no threshold. The form records pH and EC. Organic matter has no report field in this version.

## X10+ effects on leafy vegetables

- General effects only. FAO chapter 2 explains how dissolved salts reduce the water available to roots. Chapter 7 explains that pH can affect nutrient availability. The glossary gives no crop-specific range and infers no condition from a measurement.
