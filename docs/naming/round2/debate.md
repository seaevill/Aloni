Finalists: **Aloni** (bare) · **AloniField / Aloni Farm** · **Bostani / Bostani Farm** · **Mera Farm** · **Cratefield**. One extra variant entered the debate: bare brand "Aloni" with the English descriptor only in the store title. Bora's **AloniApp** idea is scored as its own row.

## Debate

**Moderator:** Availability changed the board. Bostani led round 1, but the bare name belongs to a Gulf chocolatier with live apps on both stores. Mera has the best meanings and the worst search results. Aloni is the only root with no clash and no bad meaning found. Bora also wants the name to work outside Greece. Marketing, you ranked Aloni first. Do you still?

**Marketing:** Yes, and the availability report makes the case stronger. One point on architecture. The brand must work in **both alphabets**: that is a hard requirement. "Αλώνι" works and "Aloni" works. "AloniField" has no Greek form. «ΑλώνιΦιλντ» repeats the SunField problem. So the brand is **Aloni / Αλώνι**, and the English goes where English buyers read it: the store title, the subtitle and, if needed, the domain. For international growth, a short, neutral foreign word that we fill with meaning works better than a compound that needs translating. The master brand is Aloni, and the products sit under it: the field app is just "Aloni", and Κλούβες becomes **Aloni Κλούβες** (EN "Aloni Crates").

**ASO:** Agreed, and the stores back you up. Both store titles allow 30 characters, and every character spent on "Field" or "App" is one less for a keyword. `Aloni: Spray Log & Crop Plan` is 28 characters and puts "spray log" in the indexed title. `AloniField: Spray Log & Crops` is 29 and only repeats a generic word. On **AloniApp**: Apple's guidelines discourage generic terms in the app name, "App" is never searched, and it looks amateur next to the store's own "App" label. A bare "Aloni" title may already be taken (an unidentified iOS app id6446095680 shows up in searches), but store names only need to be unique as a whole string, so "Aloni: …" solves that. No "official" or "certified" in the title.

**Social:** I'll partly disagree. A bare "aloni" handle is almost surely gone: it's an Israeli surname and the name of tavernas and an olive-oil brand. So handles will need a suffix anyway, and I want **one** suffix everywhere. X allows 15 characters and no dots. `alonifield` (10) or `alonifarm` (9) fit everywhere, and so does #alonifield as a hashtag nobody else uses. My compromise: brand **Aloni**, handle form **aloni.app** where dots are allowed and **aloni_app** on X, or **alonifield** if the first is gone. Bora's AloniApp is fine as a *handle*, not as the *name*. Mera Farm was my round-1 pick, but with Hindi "mera" flooding every feed, I can't own a hashtag there.

**TikTok:** Bostani was my baby, but a chocolatier with paid TikTok campaigns is a wall. If people search "bostani", they get pralines. Aloni works as a sound: "a-LO-ni". Hook: drone shot over an old stone αλώνι near Megara: «Εδώ μετρούσαν τη σοδειά. Τώρα τη μετράει το Αλώνι.» ("This is where they used to count the harvest. Now Aloni counts it.") That is a clip. AloniApp, honestly, is cringe. AloniField is okay but nobody says "field" in a voice note.

**Influencer:** The threshing floor is a **circle**, so the logo writes itself, and the story is communal, which fits the neighbour pilot. I lose the "one word, five languages" video I had with Bostani, but the αλώνι story is better for Greece, and the store title explains it to everyone else.

**Farmer:** Hear it from the field. Bostani is still the word Mehmet and Arben know on day one. I score it high, but I won't fight a chocolate company. Aloni: every old village on the plain has αλώνια, so Kostas gets it. Mehmet and Arben don't know the meaning, but they say "a-LO-ni" right the first time: no χ, no μπ, no ü. That passes my 6 a.m. test. "Field" is the hard part: my Albanian crew says "fild" or "fiyeld". "Farm" is easier. **Cratefield** fails: "kreyt-fild" is two English sounds my crew doesn't have, and in Greek letters it's nonsense. A mild warning on Aloni: αλωνίζω also means "to roam freely". Harmless.

**Software:** From the engineering side, `aloni` is perfect: five lowercase ASCII letters that every Greek, Turk and Albanian types the same way. Camel-case compounds break: `AloniField` ends up as `alonifield`, `aloni-field` and `aloniField` across 15 config files. Bostani is the worst here: `com.bostani.bostani` is **already a live Android package**, and Greeks type Mpostani. So: slug `aloni`, npm scope `@aloni/*`, bundle IDs under a domain we actually own. Ideally that's `app.aloni.field`, with the sibling `app.aloni.orders`. Bundle IDs are fixed forever after the first store upload (M5), but nothing before that is, so the repo rename from SunField can wait until Bora confirms.

**Marketing (to social):** I accept compound **handles and domains**, as long as the logo and the spoken name stay "Aloni".

**Social:** Deal.

**ASO (to farmer):** Turkish later: `Aloni: İlaçlama & Ekim Defteri` is exactly 30, so it fits.

**Moderator:** Bora's two examples, then. **AloniField**: a good fallback *form* (domain and handle), not the brand, because it breaks the Greek-letter rule. **AloniApp**: rejected as a name, allowed only as a handle (`aloni_app` / `aloni.app`). Final scores.

## Decision (recommendation, pending Bora's checks)

**Recommended name: Aloni / Αλώνι.**

**(a) Spelling.** Latin **Aloni** (capital A, no accent). Greek **Αλώνι** (accent on ώ). In all-caps: **ALONI / ΑΛΩΝΙ** (no accent in Greek capitals). Said a-LO-ni, with stress on the second syllable. Logo wordmark in lowercase: `aloni`.

**(b) Where the English goes.** The English word **stays out of the brand**. It goes only in the store title and subtitle, and in a domain or handle if the bare forms are taken. Reason: "AloniField" has no Greek form, which breaks a hard requirement. A neutral short word also travels better internationally than a translated compound.

**(c) Store titles (Apple and Google, ≤30 characters, spaces counted)**

| | Title | Chars |
|---|---|---|
| EN | `Aloni: Spray Log & Crop Plan` | 28 |
| EL | `Αλώνι: Βιβλίο Ψεκασμών & Σπορά` | 30 |
| EL (short alternative) | `Αλώνι: Βιβλίο Ψεκασμών` | 22 |
| TR (later) | `Aloni: İlaçlama & Ekim Defteri` | 30 |
| EN subtitle (Apple) | `Field, weather & crate orders` | 29 |
| EL subtitle (Apple) | `Χωράφι, καιρός & παραγγελίες` | 28 |

Never use "official", "certified" or "εγκεκριμένο". Never use agency names such as ΟΠΕΚΕΠΕ (Apple 2.3.1 / 2.3.7).

**(d) Domains and handles to check (in order of preference)**
- Domains: `aloni.app` → `aloni.gr` → `getaloni.com` → `alonifield.com` / `alonifield.app` → `alonifarm.com`. Also try `aloni.com.gr`. `aloni.com` is probably taken (surname).
- Handles (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, Threads): `aloni.app` → `alonifield` → `alonifarm`. On X (letters, digits, `_` only, max 15): `aloni_app` → `alonifield`. Use the **same** suffix on every platform. Hashtags: `#aloni` + `#alonifield`.

**(e) Slug and IDs.** Slug `aloni`. npm scope `@aloni/rules-core`. Cloudflare `aloni.pages.dev` (or `aloni-app` if taken). Bundle ID / Android package: **`app.aloni.field`** if Bora registers aloni.app, otherwise `gr.aloni.field` (only if he owns aloni.gr). Sibling app: `app.aloni.orders`. Fix this before the first store upload (M5). It can never change after that.

**(f) Taglines**
- EL: «Από το χωράφι στην κλούβα – σε μία οθόνη.» ("From the field to the crate, on one screen")
- TR: «Tarladan siparişe, tek ekranda.» ("From field to order, on one screen")

**(g) Κλούβες.** It becomes **Aloni Κλούβες** (EN: Aloni Crates), the orders module of the same brand, on the same developer account, domain and push keys. If the Phase 2 merge happens, it becomes the «Κλούβες» tab inside Aloni. If it stays a separate app until then: `Αλώνι Κλούβες: Παραγγελίες` (26) / `Aloni Crates: Crate Orders` (26), bundle `app.aloni.orders`. The word «Κλούβες» stays in Greek-facing text only, not in the master brand (riot-van slang risk).

**(h) Risks and Bora's checks before committing**
1. **TMview** (tmdn.org/tmview), classes 9 and 42, territories EU + GR + TR: search "ALONI" and "ALONIFIELD". Note the existing *Aloni* olive-oil brand (class 29, low risk) and any surname-based marks.
2. **Open iOS app id6446095680** (apps.apple.com/app/id6446095680) to see what "Aloni" app exists. Search "Aloni" in both stores on his iPhone.
3. **Domains**: check the list in (d) at papaki.gr or Cloudflare Registrar. Ask before buying (costs money).
4. **Handles**: check by hand on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube and X. Reserve the free ones the same day.
5. **Voice test**: 10-second voice note to 3 farmers and 2 workers (Turkish and Albanian speakers). They should hear "Aloni" once and repeat it.
6. Known soft risks: English "a loony" (different stress, rare); αλωνίζω = "roam freely" (mild); Hebrew surname (neutral); the meaning is lost on non-Greeks (neutral, so the store title has to carry it).

**(i) Runner-up.** **Bostani Farm / Μποστάνι** is the runner-up name (38). It has the best shared meaning across worker languages, but the root is held by a chocolatier in the stores and on Android. If the bare "Aloni" handles or domains are gone, the **fallback form of the winner** is **AloniField** (45) for domain and handles only, with the brand still "Aloni".
