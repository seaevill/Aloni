## Notes

1. The only name with no clash found **and** no bad meaning is **Aloni**. Its weak point is that it means less to Turkish/Albanian/Arabic speakers (neutral, not negative).
2. Bostani is the one word understood in Greek, Turkish, Albanian, Balkan Slavic and Arabic, but the bare name is not ownable in stores.
3. For international use, **Aloni + English word (AloniField / Aloni Farm)** is the cleanest found. Fully English farming names are crowded; none found was both free and easy for Greek/Turkish speakers.
4. Next steps (Bora): TMview search, classes 9 and 42 (EU + GR + TR), for Aloni, AloniField, Bostani; check domains at a registrar (papaki.gr / Cloudflare); check handles by hand.
