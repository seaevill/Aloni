# Round 1 – TikTok farm kid (22, Greek–Turkish family)

Okay so my test is simple: I say it once in a 7-second clip, can you find it in search, and does my παππούς say it right without me spelling it. Here we go.

## Ranked

**1. Bostani · Μποστάνι**
Hook: "POV: your whole bostani lives in your phone now 📱🥬"
Why: Greek μποστάνι, Turkish *bostan*, Albanian/Bulgarian/Arabic all have it. Grandpa AND my Tirana cousin say it right first try. Sounds warm, not a startup. Great as a sound: "bo-STA-ni".
Risks: Greek idiom "δεν είναι μποστάνι" (= it's not a free-for-all) – could be a meme *for* us, could be a joke at us. Some think "melon patch". Μπ → B transliteration is fine but check people don't type "Mpostani".

**2. Mera · Μέρα**
Hook: "Every μέρα starts with one screen."
Why: Greek "day" (the app literally is "what to do today"), Turkish *mera* = pasture. Hidden in "Me-ga-ra"-ish. Four letters, zero spelling fights.
Risks: too generic for search – you'll drown under "mera" hits; needs a suffix (Mera Farm / meraapp). Domains probably gone.

**3. Chorafi · Χωράφι**
Hook: "Spinach, rain, spray log – όλο το χωράφι σε ένα app."
Why: honest, Greek, every farmer gets it instantly.
Risks: Latin spelling is a mess – Chorafi / Horafi / Xorafi. Search dies there. Turks/English say "Ko-rafi". Fine for Greece only.

**4. Klouva · Κλούβα**
Hook: "Ordered 12 klouves? App already told the boys how much to sow."
Why: direct link to Κλούβες, very us.
Risks: κλούβα is also slang for the riot-police bus (ΜΑΤ). Greek comments section will cook this. Also kloova/klouva/kluva spelling split.

**5. SunField**
Hook: …honestly I can't write one that doesn't sound like a solar panel ad.
Why: easy for English speakers and the stores.
Risks: corporate, generic, sounds like a PV park or a cereal brand. Grandpa says "San-fild". Zero Megara soul.

## Honest reactions to the old list

- **Chorafi** – right vibe, wrong letters for search. Keep as a tagline maybe.
- **Tetradio** – no. Sounds like homework. Nobody's sharing a clip of their τετράδιο. (Love that it's the notebook origin story though – use it in the launch video, not the name.)
- **Kloubes Agros** – two words, two spelling problems, too long for a handle.
- **SunField** – safe, forgettable, scroll-past.

My pick: **Bostani**, with "your field, one screen" energy. Check handles @bostani.app / @bostani.gr before anyone falls in love.
