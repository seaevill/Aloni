# Naming council, round 1: marketing view (2026-11-17)

*Role: brand strategist, agri-tech in Southern Europe. Nobody has checked trademarks, domains or the app stores for these names yet. Each finalist needs an EUIPO, store and domain check.*

Test used: does the name work on a Megara kafeneio table, an Istanbul bostan and an App Store listing? Letters chosen so every language reads them the same way. No *ch/th/sh/ü*, and no Latin *b/d*, which Greek speakers read as β/δ.

## Candidates

**1. Aloni / Αλώνι**: *a-LO-nee*. Threshing floor: the round village yard where the harvest was gathered, counted and shared.
- Fit: it is the place where field work turns into counted produce, which is exactly the orders↔field link. Communal, so it carries the pilot with neighbouring growers. Family brand: *Aloni Κλούβες* (orders) and *Aloni Χωράφι* (field).
- Risks: English ear may hear "a loony" (rare, stress differs). Tavernas use the name, so look for a store or domain variant such as *aloni.app*.

**2. Bostani / Μποστάνι**: *bo-STA-nee*. Vegetable garden. One shared word across Turkish *bostan*, Bulgarian *бостан*, Albanian *bostan* and Arabic *bustān*.
- Fit: the only candidate that every worker language already knows. Istanbul's *bostan* are leafy-greens plots, which gives a ready-made bridge to Turkey.
- Risks: Greek idiom «βρήκε το μποστάνι ξέφραγο» means "found a free-for-all". To Greeks and Albanians the word suggests melons more than greens. Many businesses are called Bostan.

**3. Mera / Μέρα**: *ME-ra*. Greek "day". Turkish *mera* means pasture, Bulgarian *мера* means measure.
- Fit: the app is the "Today" screen. Three positive meanings and two syllables, and it sits inside "Me-ga-ra".
- Risks: very generic, so weak in the stores and as a trademark. It needs a qualifier such as *Mera Farm*. Spanish/Italian *mera* = "mere".

**4. Strema / Στρέμμα**: *STRE-ma*. The land unit every Greek grower counts in.
- Fit: instantly agricultural and local, and trustworthy because it is the farmer's own word.
- Risks: the *str-* cluster is hard for Arabic speakers. Turkish growers use *dönüm*, so it weakens the move to Turkey. It is descriptive, so the trademark is weak.

**5. Ora / Ώρα**: *O-ra*. "Hour, the right time", which is the spray-window promise. Albanian *ora* = hour, Bulgarian *ора* = ploughs, Turkish *ora* = there (neutral).
- Fit: short and easy to say everywhere.
- Risks: it collides with the ORA car brand. It only covers timing, not orders or the spray record.

## Ranking
1 Aloni · 2 Bostani · 3 Mera · 4 Strema · 5 Ora

## Rejected earlier names
- **SunField**: English only. In Greek letters it becomes «Σανφίλντ». Solar farms use the name everywhere. It says nothing about orders, records or Megara, and nothing to build trust with farmers.
- **Chorafi**: *Ch* is read as k, tʃ or x depending on the language. It is the plain dictionary word for "field", so it is hard to trademark and it means nothing in Turkey.
- **Tetradio**: four syllables. "Notebook" shrinks the brand to record-keeping and loses the orders→sowing story.
- **Kloubes Agros**: two words, and the spelling is wrong (β = v, so *Klouves*). Greek slang «κλούβα» also means the riot-police van. Keep Κλούβες as a sub-brand, not the master brand.
