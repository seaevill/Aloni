# Naming council, round 1: the influencer view (2026-11-17)

*Role: Greek farming/food creator (Instagram, YouTube). Test: can I say it on camera, will people tag it, does it have a story? Not checked with EUIPO, app stores or domain registrars.*

## Ranked

**1. Bostani / Μποστάνι** (bo-STA-ni)
Intro: *"Καλημέρα από το μποστάνι! Σήμερα στα Μέγαρα…"*
Why it works: one word that five languages already know. Greek μποστάνι, Turkish *bostan*, Albanian/Bulgarian *bostan* and Arabic *bustān* all mean a vegetable garden or patch. That alone is a video ("one word, five languages"). Warm, folksy; #bostani is easy to type and looks uncrowded.
Risks: it can sound old-fashioned (melon patch, not tech). Restaurants and shops may use it; needs a trademark search.

**2. Tsapa / Τσάπα** (TSA-pa)
Intro: I hit the ground with an actual hoe and say *"Τσάπα!"*
Why it works: the hoe is a perfect prop, and Turkish has the same word (*çapa*). Punchy, two syllables, meme-ready.
Risks: Turks will want to spell it Çapa (one name, two spellings). It sounds close to τσάμπα ("for free" or "worthless"): a fun pun for a free plan, or a joke against us. *Çapa* also means anchor (harmless).

**3. Mera / Μέρα** (ME-ra)
Intro: *"Τι λέει η Μέρα σήμερα;"* ("What does Mera say today?") with the Today screen held to the camera.
Why it works: in Greek *μέρα* is "day", and the Today screen is the heart of the app. In Turkish *mera* means pasture. Very short, works in both alphabets.
Risks: a common word, hard to own or search; #mera is noisy. Bulgarian *мера* = "measure" (neutral).

**4. Klouva / Κλούβα** (KLOO-va)
Intro: I hold up an empty crate: *"Μία κλούβα, μία εφαρμογή."*
Why it works: sister of Κλούβες (one family brand); the crate is a tangible prop.
Risks: in Greek slang κλούβα is the riot-police van, and κλούβιος means "rotten" or "empty-headed" (κλούβιο αυγό = rotten egg). The comments will go there.

**5. Chorafi / Χωράφι** (ho-RA-fi)
Warm and honest, but #χωράφι is already full of everyone's posts, and foreigners read "Ch" as in "chore". Fine as a tagline (*"το χωράφι σου στο κινητό"*, "your field on your phone"), weak as a brand.

## Previously floated names

- **Chorafi:** see #5. Better as a word inside the slogan than as the name.
- **Tetradio:** four syllables, sounds like homework, "tetra" suggests Tetra Pak. Nobody tags a notebook. Use it as a feature name ("Το τετράδιο ψεκασμών").
- **Kloubes Agros:** two words, too long for a hashtag; "Kloubes" is read three ways in Latin letters.
- **SunField:** nice in English, but fails the Greek-letter test (Σάνφιλντ), has no Greek/Turkish story, and is crowded by solar companies. Fine for the repo, not the brand.
