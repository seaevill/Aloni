# Round 1 – the Megara grower (2026-11-17)

*Voice: 55-year-old vegetable grower, 30 years with Greek, Turkish, Albanian and Bulgarian crews. Test: can Kostas, Mehmet and Arben all shout it across a field at 6 a.m., and will the buyer at the market not laugh?*

Safe sounds for all of us: a e i o, k l m n p r s t v. Trouble: Χ/ch, Θ/th, Δ/d, Γ/g, ü/ö, English "sh"/"th".

## Blunt on the other floated names

- **SunField** – out. No Greek-letter form (Σανφιλντ is ugly), Greeks say "San-fild". Worse: on the Megara plain "sun field" means **solar park**, the panels eating our farmland. Neighbours will think we sell photovoltaics.
- **Kloubes Agros** – out. Two words, three Latin spellings (Kloubes/Klouves/Kluves). In Greek **κλούβα** is also the riot-police bus and **κλούβιος** = rotten egg / empty-headed. Jokes guaranteed at the market. Keep "Κλούβες" for the order app only.
- **Tetradio** – easy for everyone to say, but it's a stationery-shop word and four syllables. Honest, boring, unfindable.

Nothing here is checked for domains or trademarks yet – someone must check before round 2.
