# Naming council – brief (2026-11-17)

Goal: choose the product name for the app now called "FieldWise" (taken by other companies; Bora named the GitHub repo "SunField" on 2026-11-17 – a candidate, not decided).

Read for context (pointers, don't copy): ../PROJECT-HISTORY.md, ../plan/TECH-SPEC.md (§1 only), ../reviews/3-marketing.md (naming + positioning), ../../reference/images/ (look at 1-Today.png).

What the app is: field-management PWA for small leafy-greens/herbs growers. Starts with Bora's farm on the Megara plain (Greece), then neighbouring growers (pilot 5–10), then app stores; possibly Turkey later. Differentiators: orders→sowing planning (linked to the "Κλούβες" crate-order app), inspection-ready electronic spray record (EU rule, mandatory by 1 Jan 2027), per-parcel weather advice with "Why?".

Hard requirements from Bora:
- Easy to pronounce in EVERY language his world uses: Greek, Turkish, English (also workers who may speak Albanian/Bulgarian/Arabic). Works written in Latin AND Greek letters.
- No bad/funny meaning in Greek, Turkish, English.
- Short, memorable, good for social media and app-store discovery.
Nice to have: a link to Κλούβες (sister app) or to the farm/field/Megara world; .gr/.app/.com domain and store name available.

Previously floated names: Chorafi (Χωράφι, "field"), Tetradio (Τετράδιο, "notebook"), Kloubes Agros, SunField.

## Added by Bora (2026-11-17, 13:14)
International growth matters: the name should also work outside Greece/Turkey, so English or English-compound forms should be considered, e.g. "AloniApp", "AloniField" (Bora's examples, not decisions). Derive candidates from this angle too: a local root + English word, or a fully English name, as long as it stays easy to say for Greek/Turkish/Albanian speakers.
