# Aloni usability review: real farmer workflows

Reviewer: independent usability review (dimension 1). Build: `main` 682c32d, `vite preview`, Chromium 375x812 (DPR 1, touch), Greek UI, Demo «Επίδειξη: πλήρης σεζόν (90 ημέρες)» loaded on 2026-11-28 (Athens).
Screenshots: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-aloni/12a47fa6-139c-5f71-a98a-0df4dbd0866e/scratchpad/eval/work-ux/shots/` (written below as `shots/…`). Driver scripts: `work-ux/step.js`, `work-ux/lib.js`, `work-ux/w320.js`.
Scope limits: I did not measure time on a real iPhone or in WebKit, or test sun legibility or offline use. Native date-time pickers are Chromium's, not iOS Safari's, so taps inside a picker are «unknown».

## Summary score: 5.5 / 10

**What works (with evidence)**
- **Recording a harvest is fast.** It took 7 taps for the owner: Καταγραφή συγκομιδής, planting, +10, +1, worker, destination, Καταγραφή (`shots/32-harvest-done.png`). The planting chips are grouped by field. Plantings in a waiting period show a 🔒 and the date (`shots/30-harvest-open-full.png`). Tapping a locked chip explains the reason in plain Greek (`shots/31-harvest-locked.png`).
- **Tap targets are large.** My scan for visible controls under 44 px found almost nothing. The exceptions are the «Δεν ξέρω» checkbox (13x13) in the spray form and the 81x19 «Αναζήτηση» label. Nothing scrolls sideways at 320 px on Σήμερα, Χωράφια or Φυτά (`shots/320-*.png`).
- **The spray form shows the signed label in full.** It shows the dose, PHI, REI, maximum number of applications and who signed. It also calculates the amount for the area (`Ένδειξη: 11,6 στρ. × 0,1 l = 1,16 l`). An empty save scrolls to the first missing field and lists what is missing.
- **The safety rules hold in the UI.** An unknown wait is never shown as free. Unsigned rules do not decide anything.

**Why the score is not higher**
- **Σήμερα is 3,414 px tall** (about 4.2 phone screens). The weather Bora asked to keep starts around 2,200 px down. The same fact is counted three times, in two different units (plantings vs fields). The screen shows tomorrow's orders but not today's orders.
- **Three places contradict each other.** On Today, «Μικρό Περιβόλι: Χωρίς φύτευση». In Χωράφια, the same field is «Έτοιμο για κοπή». In the harvest form you can record crates from that field, although its parsley is only *planned* for Τετ 2 Δεκ. I recorded 5 crates there as a worker and the app accepted them.
- **The record shown after saving is wrong.** After I picked «Αποθήκη» on a harvest, Σήμερα → «Σήμερα έγιναν» shows `→ άγνωστο – παλιά καταγραφή` («unknown – old record») for a record made one second earlier.
- **The worker screen does not answer «where do I go and what do I do?».** Open jobs show only an active-ingredient name, with no field or crop. «Έγινε» opens an empty start-time picker before the job can be closed. Today's jobs sit below the open jobs and the notes.
- **REI (re-entry) never appears on the owner's Σήμερα, even on a day with two sprays.** You only see it 3,600 px down a field's page, in the timeline.
