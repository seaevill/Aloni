## 1. Personas

### Senior software engineer
*I review the Preact/Dexie/Workbox code and measure the build. My questions: does state survive a reload or a deploy, is data safe in IndexedDB, what does the 2 MB precache cost.*

**What I tried:** read `main.tsx`, `ui/App.tsx`, `ui/nav.ts`, `db/index.ts`, `db/schema.ts`, `i18n/index.ts`, the forms, `vite.config.ts` and the generated `sw.js`. Reloaded 5× in worker view and sampled the first 500 ms of each render. Loaded the demo and reloaded. Simulated a deploy by changing `sw.js` on a copy of dist and calling `registration.update()`. Opened two tabs. Ran a cold load on Slow 3G with 4× CPU throttling (CDP). Measured raw and gzip sizes of every precached file.

**What delighted me:** decimals are parsed as exact text (`parseDecimal`, BigInt math, `1.000` refused). Athens time is handled carefully: I tested Europe/Athens, Asia/Kolkata and America/New_York at 23:45 and 00:15 and on the DST night, and the date and the harvest default time were always correct Athens time. The map (MapLibre, terra-draw) and the demo data are lazy-loaded. A repeat visit through the service worker paints in **0.26 s**. Offline reload and offline save both work. `navigator.storage.persist()` is requested at boot. `_headers` sets a strict CSP and no-cache on `sw.js` and the HTML.

**What blocked or confused me:**
- **Why "the view keeps vanishing" (Bora's complaint).** The worker view itself survives reloads: it's stored in `settings.worker_view`, and my 5 reloads stayed in worker view with no owner flash. The rest of the navigation state is **memory-only**: `tab` (nav.ts), `activeFarm` (always reset to the real farm in `initDb`), and every open sub-screen or form (`useState`). Two things then wipe it:
  1. **Every deploy reloads the open app.** `registerType: 'autoUpdate'` plus `skipWaiting`/`clientsClaim`, and vite-plugin-pwa's `register.js` calls `window.location.reload()` on `activated`. I reproduced it: demo farm open, deploy simulated, page reloaded by itself, and I landed on "Το κτήμα μου" (empty) on the Today tab. With 5+ PRs merged a day (#172–#176), Bora's phone gets reset several times a day.
  2. **A reload drops the demo farm**, and the "Worker view (demo)" entry in Άλλα only exists while the demo farm is active. So after a reload, the way into worker view disappears, and it comes back once he taps "Φόρτωση επίδειξης". The worker view also restores `activeFarm = demo` for itself, but the owner screens don't. The behaviour is inconsistent.
- **Data loss in forms:** the same automatic reload throws away any half-typed spray, harvest or stock form. Nothing is saved as a draft.
- **Double submits:** none of the 17 save handlers I grepped has an in-flight guard (only `LabelUseForm` has a `busy` flag). Records are append-only, so a duplicate can't be deleted, only amended (see QA).
- **Bundle and precache:** raw 2.1 MB is about 690 KB gzipped. maplibre-gl.js is 1,057 KB (51%, 284 KB gz). index.js is 418 KB (126 KB gz), and about 120 KB of that is **all four i18n JSONs bundled eagerly**. terra-draw is 266 KB, the Noto Devanagari font 121 KB, the demo `data.json` chunk 45 KB, the maplibre CSS 70 KB. `icon-*.png` and `manifest.webmanifest` appear twice in the precache list (from `includeAssets` and from `globPatterns`). That's harmless but untidy. Every phone, worker phones included, downloads the 1.3 MB of map code even though workers never see a map.
- **First load on Slow 3G with 4× CPU throttling:** first contentful paint at 4.0 s, tab bar visible at 4.4–4.6 s, network idle at 7.2 s. The service worker's precache download isn't throttled by page-level CDP, so on a real slow connection expect roughly 15–25 s before the app is fully available offline. A worker who goes out of signal in that window has no offline app.
- **Two tabs:** a parcel saved in tab A only appears in tab B after a tab switch. `dataVersion` is per tab, and there's no `BroadcastChannel` or Dexie `liveQuery`. On iOS this matters less (the home-screen app is single-instance) but it's a source of confusion when testing on a desktop.
- **Schema upgrades:** no `db.on('versionchange')` or `on('blocked')` handler. When a new version adds `this.version(9)` while an old tab is open, the upgrade can block or the old tab can lose its connection. The autoUpdate reload currently hides this by accident.
- **Day rollover:** with the app left open across midnight, Today still showed "Πέμ 19 Νοε" until another tab was tapped (`clock.runFor`). An iPhone left on Today overnight shows yesterday's date and yesterday's "today's jobs" in the morning.

### QA tester
*I try to break forms and flows with bad input, fast taps, the back button, clock changes and lost network.*

**What I tried:** parcel names and areas: empty, emoji, `-3`, `1.000`, `0`, `99999999999`, a very long Greek word, `<img onerror>`. Harvest crates: `-3`, `0`, `2,5`, emoji, `99999`, empty, double-click, triple click. Product double-click. Stock quantities: `-5`, `1,250`, `1.250`, `0`, `1e3`, `5 L`. Back button. Offline save. Two tabs. Three time zones and the midnight and DST boundaries.

**What delighted me:** validation messages are clear Greek ("Μη έγκυρο εμβαδόν. Γράψτε π.χ. 0,6 ή 12 (όχι 1.000)"). XSS input is rendered as plain text. Emoji names work. The time-zone logic is solid. Offline saves show "Εκτός σύνδεσης · Αποθηκεύτηκε στο τηλέφωνο".

**What blocked or confused me:**
- **Double-tap creates duplicate append-only records.** On the real farm I entered 4 crates of Σπανάκι and double-tapped «Καταγραφή»: **2 `job_harvest` rows**. A triple click gave 3 rows. Today then showed "Κλούβες που κόπηκαν: 20" when the truth was 8 (shot `05`). Double-tapping «Αποθήκευση» on a new product gave 2 identical products (`Δοκιμή 🧪` twice). Workers in gloves will double-tap.
- **Area has no upper bound:** `99999999999` στρ. was accepted, and it's shown without a thousands separator. A planting of 5 στρ. was accepted in a 3 στρ. parcel. There is a `planting-warn` testid, but it didn't show on the list.
- **`1,250` is refused for stock quantity** (it's a normal Greek way to write 1.25 L), and the error says "Γράψτε ποσότητα μεγαλύτερη από 0". The parcel form explains the `1.000` ambiguity, but the stock form doesn't.
- **Crates `0`** is refused, but no crate-specific message appeared (my regex didn't catch one). **`99999`** gets the generic "write an integer" message instead of "too many".
- **Back button:** `goBack()` from a parcel detail left the app (`about:blank`). There's no history integration at all. On the workers' Android phones, the hardware Back button closes the app in the middle of a form.
- **Stale toast:** "Αποθηκεύτηκε στο τηλέφωνο" from the previous save is still shown above a form that just failed validation. The backup nag "Δεν υπάρχει πρόσφατο αντίγραφο (πάνω από 7 ημέρες ή ποτέ)" appears on the very first record.
- **Today, "Σημερινές δουλειές":** it lists overdue jobs with an ISO date ("Ahmet · 2026-11-18") instead of "Τετ 18 Νοε". In worker view those jobs appear under "today" with **no date at all**.
- **Demo worker view:** every spray job is locked ("Άγνωστο διάστημα επανεισόδου") because the demo REI is unknown, so the demo can't show the happy path (✓ Έγινε). The lock message is rendered **twice per job**: once above the buttons and once below after tapping (shot `07`).
- **Demo stock:** the Ουρία card shows "200 kg" and "Υπόλοιπο: 0 kg" with no label on the first number (the legacy `qty` against the movement balance) (shot `06`).

### PWA specialist
*I check installability and long-term storage on iOS Safari and Android: manifest, icons, standalone mode, safe areas, update flow, eviction.*

**What I tried:** read the manifest, `index.html`, `_headers` and the CSS. Checked icon alpha and the maskable safe zone. Ran with an iPhone user agent. Checked `storage.persisted()`. Tested landscape, input font sizes (iOS zooms on fonts under 16 px) and the update flow.

**What delighted me:** `display: standalone`, `viewport-fit=cover`, `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` on the tab bar, main area and toast. The apple-touch-icon is 180 px and opaque (no black corners). All text inputs are 16 px, so iOS won't zoom. iOS users who haven't installed the app get a clear warning ("Προσθέστε την εφαρμογή στην Οθόνη Αφετηρίας…"). There's a persistence warning, and backup export/restore exists. No layout overflow in landscape.

**What blocked or confused me:**
- **No update prompt.** The update is a silent hard reload (see the engineer section). There's no "Νέα έκδοση – Ενημέρωση" banner and no protection for an open form.
- **No iOS splash screens** (`apple-touch-startup-image`), so a launch shows a plain background flash. That's acceptable but unpolished.
- There's no `<meta name="mobile-web-app-capable">` (Chrome warns that the apple-only tag is deprecated). The manifest has no `id`, which makes future `start_url` changes risky, and no `screenshots`/`orientation`.
- The maskable icon reuses the "any" artwork. The crate's corners (about 215 px from the centre) fall outside the 205 px safe circle and get clipped by round Android masks.
- **Detection gaps:** iPads are missed because iPadOS reports a "Macintosh" UA, and the `/iPad|iPhone|iPod/` check gets `ios=false`. `persist()` is called once at boot and never again after installing to the Home Screen. Safari grants persistence more readily after installation, so re-request it when `display-mode: standalone` is first seen.
- **Separate storage:** on iOS, Safari and the Home Screen app have separate storage. If Bora sets up the worker view or the demo in Safari and then opens the icon, he sees a different, empty state. This alone can look like "it vanished". The app doesn't say which container it's running in.
- **Language is per device** (`localStorage`). On a shared farm phone, switching to हिन्दी for a worker also switches Bora's owner screens into mixed Hindi/Greek (shot `12`). There's no language switch inside the worker view (shot `11`). In Hindi, labels without a translation ("Δόση:", "στρ.") stay Greek and the dose shows `0.2` instead of `0,2`.
- Landscape: no `safe-area-inset-left/right`, so the notch can cover the start of the text in landscape.

## 3. Top 5 decisions for the meeting (ranked)

1. **Stop silent reloads on deploy (E1).** Switch to a prompt-based update that never interrupts an open form. This is almost certainly what Bora experiences as "it keeps vanishing". Make it the first PR tomorrow.
2. **One `useSubmit` guard on every save button (E3)**, plus a Playwright double-click test. Duplicates in append-only spray and harvest records are a compliance and data-quality problem we can't clean up afterwards.
3. **Route state into the URL hash (E2 + E5).** Tab, farm and the open detail survive reloads, and Back works on the workers' Android phones. A single change fixes both.
4. **Storage honesty on iOS (E17):** re-request `persist()` after install, show "Οθόνη Αφετηρίας vs Safari" in Άλλα, and tell Bora that Safari and the icon don't share data. Pair it with the existing backup nag.
5. **Slim the worker phone (E6 + E19):** take the map out of the precache, load language files on demand, and give each worker their own language so a shared phone doesn't switch Bora's screens to Hindi.
