## Worker requirements (from `docs/team-reviews/2026-11-17/worker.md` §4; inputs, to be confirmed in the M2 plan)
1. **Safety status at a glance, without words.** Each job card: big icon + colour (never colour alone) + a countdown («Μην μπείτε – 2 ώρ. 15 λ.» / «Για κοπή σήμερα»). The abbreviations REI/ΠΧΑ are **never shown to workers**. During the re-entry period the card and the counter are fully locked. Wording must not promise safety (see `docs/i18n/glossary-el.md`).
2. **Send status like WhatsApp.** Per record: saved on the phone ✓ (grey) → reached Bora ✓✓ (green). Recording works without signal; pressing twice never creates a duplicate (idempotent op ids); pending records are counted.
3. **Language + pictures.** Choice **el / tr / sq (Albanian)** on first launch; a photo/icon next to every parcel and crop; reading a job card aloud (candidate).
4. **Gloves.** Counter and Done/Skip buttons **72 px**, at the bottom; vibration on every press; **10 s undo**; Skip reasons as icon chips (no penalty wording: "skip" is not a punishment). Owner screens stay at 56 px.
5. **Privacy and easy sign-in.** First-launch screen in the worker's language: **"your location is not tracked; Bora sees: your jobs, times, crate counts"**; the worker sees their own history; forgotten PIN reset by Bora in one tap; new phone = QR re-login.

Ideas in the review that are **not** committed (decide in the M2 plan): voice messages for problem reports, photo reports, read-aloud, field names as nicknames.
