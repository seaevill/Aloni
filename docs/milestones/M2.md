## 1. Goal
Bora (and his workers) record what really happened in the field, on the phone, offline: **spray log** with label checks and automatic PHI/REI, **harvest counter** with the PHI gate, other jobs (fertilise, irrigate, sow, observations), and a **worker Today** screen on each worker's own phone. Everything reaches Bora's phone when there is signal.

## 2. Prerequisite track (M2-0, before any logging code)
Workers on their own phones cannot work on the M1 local-only data model: they need accounts and sync.
1. **Supabase set up by Bora** (RUNBOOK §9: Frankfurt, Free plan; he never sends keys). Migrations 0001–0006 are pasted into the SQL Editor by him, one by one.
2. **PR 5 – SQL `apply_ops` and pull** (idempotent by `op_id`, pull by `server_seq`, RLS), tested on CI Postgres against the phone-shaped rows of migration 0006.
3. **PR 16 – live sync transport** (`SYNC_CONNECTED = true`: the old «N μη συγχρονισμένα» wording returns, per record «saved on the phone ✓ → reached Bora ✓✓»), photo upload (reads the bytes from the phone, checks the SHA-256).
4. **Auth + invitations:** owner sign-in; **worker accounts invited by the owner**, PIN login on the worker's own phone, new phone = QR re-login, forgotten PIN reset by Bora in one tap, a lost phone is switched off by Bora (`active = false`). **The invite is closed until Bora has entered the data controller's name, address and phone** (`canInviteWorkers()` gate from M1); the worker sees the notice in his language at first launch: "your location is not tracked; Bora sees your jobs, times and crate counts".
5. **Worker view of the data:** RLS lets a worker read only parcels, crops, plantings and his own jobs; no stock, products' label values for pricing, orders or other workers' records. Worker phones hold only that.

## 3. Scope (in)
**Spray log** (append-only `job_spray`, hash chain on the server, corrections = amending rows):
- Pick plantings (parcel + crop), product, lot; the app **shows** the label values for that product × crop. It **never chooses a product or a dose** (CLAUDE.md rule 2).
- **Blocks (no way to save as «planned/allowed»):** no label use for product × crop, label incomplete («ΑΠΟΚΛΕΙΣΜΕΝΟ»), no agronomist's signature, expired lot, not enough stock, dose outside the signed range, dose 0.
- **Warnings that need a written reason:** maximum applications reached, same FRAC/IRAC group again, PHI would end after the planned harvest start.
- **PHI/REI** computed only by `phiEnd` / `reiEnd` from M1 (exact seconds, never shorter, never rounded; unknown = blocked). Only a `SignedLabelUse` may feed deciding code.
- **Stock:** the spray writes its `out` movement through the M1 ledger in the same atomic `putMany` (a spray never exists without its movement, and the reverse).
**Harvest counter** (`job_harvest`, crates): blocked while a PHI is running for that planting («σε αναμονή», countdown), never a «safe» wording.
**Other logs:** fertilise, irrigate, sow, observations (with photo). Simple forms, same append-only rule.
**Worker Today:** assigned job cards (parcel photo/icon, crop, what to do), icon + colour + countdown for waiting periods, **REI-locked card** («Μην μπείτε – 2 ώρ. 15 λ.»), big buttons (**72 px**, bottom of the screen, vibration, **10 s undo**), skip with icon reasons (no punishment wording), history of his own jobs. The words ΠΧΑ/REI never appear for workers.
**Assignment:** Bora assigns jobs to workers; a worker may complete, skip, log a harvest and an observation; he cannot edit stock, products or label values.
**Audit and export:** audit trail of every record, Greek PDF/CSV of the spray record. **The app drafts the record; a person signs it** (the official inspection template is asked from the agronomist).

## 4. Out of scope (stays later)
Weather, rules, recommendations, notifications (M3/M4); costs; Κλούβες sync; voice messages, read-aloud, nicknames for fields (ideas, not committed); sensor data; multi-farm; app stores. **Labels are still typed by Bora from the product label; no OCR, no prefilling.**

## 7. Safety rules carried into M2 (never break)
1. Never invent label data; missing = blocked. 2. The app suggests; it never chooses a product or dose, never shortens a PHI/REI, never signs. 3. Unsigned values may be shown but never decide. 4. Demo farm stays isolated. 5. No tracking, no extra personal data, EU hosting only.
**The permanent condition is met** (M1.md §9): `Known<T>`, `SignedLabelUse`, `parseDecimal`, the safe-side tests and the property tests are on `main`; M2 code must use `phiEnd` / `reiEnd` / `waitOver` (no second implementation) and add its own safe-side tests.

## 8. Risks
- **Sync is the hard part** (conflicts, restore, iOS PWA storage); hence M2-0 first and the restore rules fixed in M1 close.
- **iOS** may drop a Home Screen app's storage; the backup banner and worker re-login cover it.
- **Label data is typed by hand**: a mistake is possible; the agronomist's signature on exact values is the control, and any edit drops it.
- **Legal:** worker notice, controller details, lawyer review of the drafts (still marked «προς έλεγχο από δικηγόρο») before the first real worker is invited.
