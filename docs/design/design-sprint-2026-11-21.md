# Aloni design sprint, 2026-11-21: meeting record and work orders

Inputs: CLAUDE.md, `app/src/ui/tokens.css`, `docs/design/theme-2026-11/` (theme B), `reference/prototype/` (v2 = layout target), the gap audit `docs/design/v2-gap-audit.md` (branch `codex/20261121-v2-gap-audit`), the eight `codex/20261121-v2-*` branches, the before/after PNGs in `scratchpad/review/` (crops, crop detail, parcels, parcel detail, stock, register, more, spray form), `docs/design/compare/{today,worker}.png`, and the secondary canvas `docs/design/target-fieldwise-megara/`.
Hard limits for everything below: theme B identity; text contrast ≥7:1; touch targets 56 px (72 px for worker actions); Greek first; readable in sun and with gloves; never "safe"/«ασφαλές»; no diagnosis; no choice of product or dose; no invented numbers (a missing value is hidden or shown as «άγνωστο»); unsigned rules are info only; demo data stays in the Demo farm; no tracking.

## 0. Critique round: what the screenshots show

- **Product/UI designer (Eleni).** "Every screen has the same skeleton: a forest band, a grey intro sentence, then white cards *inside* white cards. On Καλλιέργειες the crop row is a white pill with a shadow, sitting inside a white card with a shadow. That's two elevations for a single thing, and it's why it reads as a form, not a product. (1) No nested cards, ever. A card holds rows, and rows are flat. (2) The forest band shows up on every screen, so the hero on Σήμερα loses its punch. Forest should mean 'look here'. (3) The Codex 'after' shots are mostly the same layout with more text in it. Stock and the parcel tiles got *longer*. v2's strength is the big numbers and the short labels, and none of that came across yet."
- **Graphic designer (Manos, icons).** "(1) The stock warnings show a stray backtick (`) where the icon should be. That's a broken glyph sitting in a safety message. (2) Άλλα got its first icons from Codex in tinted tiles, which is good. But they're one-offs, and the tab icons are inline paths in App.tsx. We need one 24 px set with a 2 px stroke and round caps, using `currentColor`. (3) The «×» in the lime pill on the Σήμερα hero looks like a close button. Status icons can't borrow UI-control glyphs."
- **Brand artist (Daphne).** "Right now the app has no face. There's no wordmark, and the icon PNGs are placeholders. An αλώνι is a circular stone threshing floor. A ring of 12 stones round a centre works as the logo, as the loader, as the empty-state frame and as a share-card frame. It's provisional, so build it from one SVG source. When the name changes, we swap one file."
- **Painter/illustrator (Yiannis).** "Bora grows 17 crops, and Σέσκουλα and Ραδίκια look identical in a text list. Give every crop a small hand-drawn portrait: one forest line, one lime-soft fill, a slightly wobbly line so it doesn't look clip-art. Empty states get the same hand. Nothing cute, nothing with faces."
- **Photographer (Petros).** "No photos in the chrome. In Megara sun a photo behind text is unreadable, it's heavy offline, and stock photos would be a lie about his farm. Photos are *evidence*: the worker snaps a leaf or a crate, and the photo stays attached to a record. That capture flow is where I want effort: a 72 px shutter, auto-tagged to the job, and EXIF GPS stripped before anything leaves the phone."
- **Motion designer (Ilias).** "Motion only confirms an action. It never asks for attention. «Έγινε» gets a check that draws in 280 ms *after* the save is already committed. Nothing pulses, and nothing loops. Under reduced motion it's opacity only."
- **Social / influencer (Kat, 'Twoislandsdallas').** "A share-worthy screenshot is one big number, one crop drawing and the date, all on sage, with the ring mark. 'This week: 340 κλούβες' with a hand-drawn spinach is a post. The weather grid is not. The owner taps the share himself, sees a preview, and the image carries no worker names, no field names or location, no spray data, no prices and no client names. If the Demo farm is selected, it's watermarked «ΕΠΙΔΕΙΞΗ»."
- **Farmer (Bora).** "I open the app at 6:30 and I want three things: what's blocked, how many crates I can cut, and whether orders are covered. The scientific names in bold on every crop are noise to me. On Χωράφια I want to *see my fields next to each other*, not five separate boxes. And the red dashed lines on Codex's sketches look like something is wrong."
- **Worker (Ahmet, veto).** "Gloves, sun, one hand. I veto: (a) anything I have to wait through before I can tap, (b) swipe gestures, (c) pictures above my job card, (d) moving «Έγινε» away from the bottom, (e) a tutorial. The register buttons that wrap mid-word («προειδοποιήσεω ν»), and the 16 tiny «crop · field» buttons in the spray form, slow Bora down too."

Debate outcomes: forest bands on every screen vs hero-only → **hero-only won** (Eleni and Kat for, Bora neutral). Combined parcel canvas vs a grid of tiles → **one canvas won** (Bora). Tab label «Φυτά» (as in v2) vs «Καλλιέργειες» → «Φυτά» won, because «Καλλιέρ-γειες» breaks in the 75 px column. The tab bar only, though: screen titles keep «Καλλιέργειες». Animated weather → rejected (Ahmet's veto: battery, and no information in it).

## 1. Visual direction: 12 decisions

1. **Surfaces and elevation.** Two levels only. `--bg` sage is the page. `Card` is white, `--r-card` 22 px, `--shadow-card`, with 16 px padding. Rows inside a card are **flat**: no background, a 1 px `--line` divider and a 56 px minimum height. Cards never nest. A new `HeroCard` (forest `#134A2B`, white text, optional lime fill pill) is the **only** forest surface below the header, at most one per screen.
2. **Page header.** `PageHead` puts a 30/800 title on sage (`--ls-title`) with an optional 16/500 `--muted` subtitle and a back row. The forest band stays **only on Σήμερα and the worker screen**, where it carries the date. The glass top bar appears when the page scrolls (≥8 px) and holds the 18/800 title. The demo intro sentence moves into the single Demo ribbon (decision 12).
3. **Type scale (tokens).** `--fs-hero:44px` (big numerals), `--fs-title:30px`, `--fs-h2:22px`, `--fs-row:18px`, `--fs-body:16px`, `--fs-meta:14px`. **The 14 px floor applies everywhere, including the tab labels and the sketch labels.** The Codex sketch tiles' ~11 px text gets fixed. Numerals use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`, and a number and its unit are joined by U+00A0. Scientific names drop to 14/400 italic `--muted`, on crop detail only.
4. **Big numerals and crate data viz.** `BigNum` sets the value 44/800 in `--fg`, the unit 18/700, and a 14/600 label above. A missing value shows «άγνωστο» at 22/700 `--muted`, never 0 and never «—» alone. `Sparkline` is 120×32, with a 2 px forest stroke, no axes and no fill. The last point is a 6 px lime dot with a 2 px forest ring, and the line always sits beside a text label. It plots **logged** crates only. `WeekBars` (orders vs supply, 4 weeks): the supply bar is a forest fill 20 px high with `--r-sm`. Held/unknown supply is a separate 45° hatched segment (`--line` on white), labelled «άγνωστο», and it is never added to the total. Orders are a 3 px `--fg` vertical marker with the number above it. Short/extra is stated in text with an icon («▼ λείπουν 20 κλούβες»), never by colour alone.
5. **Status chip system.** `Chip` is 32 px high (non-interactive), 14/700, radius pill, a 16 px icon and 10 px horizontal padding. The **icon, word and fill all differ** between tones:

   | tone | bg / ink | icon | example text |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | `hold` | `#FCE3DC` / `#6E1708` | hourglass | «Αναμονή λήγει Δευ 23 Νοε» |
   | `blocked` | `#6E1708` / `#FFF` | lock | «ΑΠΟΚΛΕΙΣΜΕΝΟ» |
   | `warn` | `#FFEDC2` / `#432C00` | clock | «Κοπή από Πέμ 3 Δεκ» |
   | `unsigned` | `#FFEDC2` / `#432C00` | pen-off | «Χωρίς υπογραφή γεωπόνου» |
   | `frost` | `#E1ECFA` / `#0A2C52` | snowflake | «Παγετός απόψε» |
   | `ready` | `#EAF8C4` / `#134A2B` | sickle | «Έτοιμο για κοπή» (only when rules-core reports a documented wait that has expired) |
   | `unknown` | `#FFF`, 2 px dashed `--line` / `#2D4033` | question | «Αναμονή: άγνωστο» |
   | `info` | `#FFF`, 1 px `--line` / `#2D4033` | info | «Πληροφοριακό» |

   There is no green check badge anywhere. Neutral metadata (FRAC 28, Λίπασμα) uses `Tag`: 28 px, 14/600, `--line` border, no icon, so it never looks like a status.
6. **Icon set.** About 32 icons on a 24 px grid: 2 px stroke, round caps and joins, `currentColor`, no fills except the dot. They're inlined as tiny Preact components from `app/src/ui/icons/`. Set: today, crops (leaf), parcels (map), stock (box), more, back, chevron, plus, check, x-mark (dismiss only), hourglass, lock, clock, pen-off, snowflake, sickle, question, info, wind, rain, drop, thermometer, crate, sprayer, camera, share, export, print, users, worker, photo, calendar, sync, offline. The «×» never appears as a status icon. `IconTile` is 40 px, `--calm-bg` fill, forest icon, `--r-md`.
7. **Crop illustration set (17, original).** 48 px viewBox, a single 2 px forest stroke (`#134A2B`) with a slightly irregular hand line, plus **one** `#EAF8C4` fill shape and no other colours. Files are `app/src/ui/illustrations/crops/<slug>.svg.tsx`. Sizes: 40 px in a 56 px lime-soft tile (lists), 96 px (crop detail hero), 160 px (share card). Motifs: Σπανάκι arrow-leaf rosette · Ρόκα deeply lobed leaf · Μαϊντανός flat triple leaflet · Άνηθος umbel plus thread leaves · Κόλιανδρο round scalloped leaves · Ραδίκια άγρια toothed dandelion rosette · Αντίδια frilly curled rosette · Σέσκουλα broad leaf with a thick white rib · Κρεμμύδι φρέσκο three tubes over a small white bulb · Πράσο fan of flat blades on a white shank · Βλήτα oval leaves with a seed tassel · Καυκαλήθρα lobed basal leaf with a small umbel · Λάχανα round head with an outer leaf · Μυρώνι feathery leaf plus needle-like seed pods · Παντζάρια round root with red-veined stems (drawn as lines) · Ραπάνι round root with a tail and two leaves · Σέλινο ribbed stalks with leaf tops. The drawings are decorative (`aria-hidden`), and the crop name is always printed beside them. They never stand for health, disease or readiness.
8. **Parcel sketch style (one canvas).** A single SVG on a sage canvas (`--sketch-h` 220 px on the list, 260 px on the detail) places all parcels relative to each other. Demo parcels use the `sketch` geometry. A real farm uses the recorded `boundary`, projected; parcels without a boundary are listed under the map as «χωρίς όρια». Each parcel has a white fill and a 2 px forest stroke, and its label is 14/700 in a white pill with `--r-pill`. The selected parcel gets a lime fill and a 3 px forest stroke. **No red, no dashes, no status colours on the shapes.** Status lives in the chip in the list row below. Every shape is a ≥56 px tap target (an invisible hit area is padded around small shapes). The caption on a Demo farm reads «Σκαρίφημα – όχι μετρημένα όρια».
9. **Empty states and onboarding.** Each empty state is a 160 px illustration (same hand as the crops, the ring motif as ground), a 20/800 headline, one 16 px sentence and **one** 56 px primary action. Eight states: no parcels, no plantings, no stock, empty register, no jobs today (worker), no orders imported, offline, no photos. Owner first-run onboarding is three skippable cards: «Το Αλώνι σου», «Κτήμα επίδειξης = φανταστικά στοιχεία», «Η εφαρμογή προτείνει· εσύ αποφασίζεις». It ends with the existing CreateFarm or Demo choice and stores only a local seen-flag. **Workers get no onboarding** (Ahmet's veto), only the language pick.
10. **Brand: wordmark, app icon, splash.** The wordmark «Αλώνι» is set in Commissioner 800, −0.03em, forest, with the Latin «Aloni» at 14/600 below it in store contexts only. The mark is a ring of 12 rounded "stones" (lime fill, 1.5 px forest seams) around a forest centre disc. The app icon is the mark on a forest square, with a separate maskable variant at 80% safe zone. The splash and manifest use `background_color #EDF2E8` and `theme_color #134A2B`. Everything comes from one `app/src/ui/brand/mark.svg` source and a script that renders 180/192/512 px and maskable. Because the name is provisional, it is read from `config.ts` and nothing else hard-codes it.
11. **Motion rules.** Tokens: `--dur-1:120ms`, `--dur-2:200ms`, `--dur-3:280ms`, `--ease:cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)`. Allowed: button press scale .97 (`--dur-1`), the «Έγινε» check-draw (`--dur-3`, after the write is committed), the crate counter tick (`--dur-2` vertical roll), a sheet slide-up (`--dur-3`), a chip fade-in (`--dur-2`). Forbidden: pulsing or blinking alerts, loops, parallax, skeleton shimmer, animated hold or blocked cards, and anything that delays input. With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, every transform is removed and fades are ≤120 ms.
12. **One Demo ribbon.** The three demo marks we have now (a pill, a header badge and an intro sentence) become one `DemoRibbon`: a full-width amber `#FFEDC2` / `#432C00` strip, 14/700 with an info icon, «Επίδειξη – φανταστικά στοιχεία», sticky under the top bar. It is never dismissable on demo data, and it is also rendered inside the share card.

**Adjustments to the proposed split, and why:**
1. `screens.tsx` currently holds both the shared helpers and the `Parcels()` list, and the Codex parcels branch edits it. Fix: Codex moves `Parcels()` into `Parcels.tsx` (C1, its first PR). After that, Codex never touches `screens.tsx`.
2. Both agents need new strings, and parallel edits to `el.json` conflict on every merge. Fix: Claude Code adds keys only to the new `ui.*.json` files (merged at load, prefix `ui.`). Codex keeps the existing JSONs. Claude Code doesn't edit existing keys. To change existing wording it adds a new `ui.` key and switches its own component to it.
3. `docs/DECISIONS.md` is append-only for both agents, so it conflicts too. Single writer: Claude Code.
4. `Photos.tsx` (worker capture UX) wasn't assigned; it goes to Claude Code with the worker screen.
5. The 14-day weather in `CropDetail` is rebuilt by Codex out of the shared `WeatherStrip` (D4), so the weather looks the same everywhere.

Until a component lands, Codex keeps the existing markup and adds `// TODO(ds): <Component>` plus a line in `docs/handoff/codex-notes.md`. Order of work: D1–D4 land first (target: within ~2 h). C1 and C11–C12 don't need them and can start right away.

## 5. Rejected ideas

- **Dark mode:** it halves the contrast headroom in direct sun and doubles QA, for no field benefit.
- **Photos as hero or background imagery:** unreadable in glare, heavy offline, and stock or staged photos would misrepresent the farm.
- **Glassmorphism on cards:** theme B keeps glass to the bars; translucent cards fail 7:1 over busy content.
- **Auto-posting or scheduled social posts:** too much privacy risk and it's out of scope. Sharing is always an owner tap with a preview.
- **Share analytics, pixels or UTM tracking:** forbidden (no tracking).
- **AI-generated or traced illustrations:** licence and authenticity risk; all art is original, simple SVG.
- **Green "✓ safe/ready" badges or traffic-light-only statuses:** banned wording, and colour alone fails colour-blind users.
- **Disease or pest icons driven by weather:** they imply a diagnosis; conditions are text only.
- **Pulsing or blinking alert cards:** they cause panic taps and break reduced motion.
- **Swipe-to-complete for workers:** fails with gloves and wet screens (worker veto).
- **Worker onboarding or tutorial:** slows the first job (worker veto).
- **Satellite or tile basemap under the parcel canvas:** the provider is still an open decision, and it costs data; the sketch canvas is enough for now.
- **Gamification (streaks, badges, confetti):** it's a work log, not a game, and it trivialises safety records.
- **3D or isometric crop art, emoji icons:** inconsistent across phones and illegible at 40 px.
- **Replacing Commissioner with a display face for the wordmark:** one family keeps it light; Greek coverage is already verified.
- **Lime-coloured text:** fails contrast on white and sage (theme B rule).
